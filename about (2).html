Zinnia Labs is a self-funded research and engineering environment focused on artificial intelligence, autonomous systems, digital architecture, and applied scientific work.

01 / PURPOSE

Build what is useful. Publish what survives scrutiny.

The work begins with a simple premise: technology should be understandable, testable, and useful beyond the presentation layer. Zinnia Labs develops research, architectures, software, and technical publications with that standard in mind.

Our work spans AI engineering, systems architecture, digital design, applied research, and experimental implementations. When a conventional answer is sufficient, we use it. When it is not, we build something better.

Zinnia Labs operates within the broader Zinnia ecosystem while preserving the independence required for research, publication, and technical exploration.

02 / PRINCIPLES

A small set of rules governs the work.

01

Evidence over theater

Claims should be traceable to implementation, mathematics, experiments, source material, or clearly identified reasoning.

02

Architecture before ornament

Systems are designed around constraints, interfaces, failure modes, and long-term operability before visual or promotional concerns.

03

Useful work should remain accessible

Whenever practical, research artifacts, tools, technical material, and reference implementations are made available without unnecessary barriers.

HIGH-IMPORTANCE SIGNAL

Accent color is reserved for information that materially changes meaning, status, risk, or required action. It is not decoration.
