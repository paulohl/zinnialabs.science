# Book Landing Page Template (WPBakery Structure)

This crisp, objective layout is designed specifically to convert visitors into book buyers. Use standard WPBakery rows, columns, and elements to build this structure without needing paid add-ons.

---

## SECTION 1: The Hero (Above the Fold)
*Purpose: Capture attention and state the book's value immediately.*

### Row 1: 2-Column Split (50% / 50%)
*   **Column 1 (Left): Visual Assets**
    *   *Element:* Single Image (Your book cover). 
    *   *Settings:* Large size, centered alignment, drop shadow enabled.
*   **Column 2 (Right): Pitch & Action**
    *   *Element:* Custom Heading (H1). Text: "[Insert Catchy Hook or Book Title]"
    *   *Element:* Text Block. Text: "A 1-sentence subtitle explaining exactly who this book is for and the main problem it solves."
    *   *Element:* Call to Action (CTA) Button. Text: "Get Your Copy Now". 
    *   *Settings:* Large size, high-contrast color (e.g., orange or deep blue), linked to your retail platform.

---

## SECTION 2: The Core Value Proposition
*Purpose: Build desire by outlining exactly what the reader will gain.*

### Row 2: 1-Column Header
*   **Column 1:** Custom Heading (H2), centered. Text: "What You Will Discover Inside"

### Row 3: 3-Column Benefits (33% / 33% / 33%)
*   **Column 1:**
    *   *Element:* Icon Box. Select a relevant flat icon (e.g., a checkmark).
    *   *Element:* Custom Heading (H3). Text: "Key Takeaway 1"
    *   *Element:* Text Block. Text: "A concise 2-line description of a powerful skill, lesson, or chapter theme."
*   **Column 2:**
    *   *Element:* Icon Box.
    *   *Element:* Custom Heading (H3). Text: "Key Takeaway 2"
    *   *Element:* Text Block. Text: "A concise 2-line description focusing on a major transformation or result."
*   **Column 3:**
    *   *Element:* Icon Box.
    *   *Element:* Custom Heading (H3). Text: "Key Takeaway 3"
    *   *Element:* Text Block. Text: "A concise 2-line description highlighting a unique framework or story."

---

## SECTION 3: Social Proof & Authority
*Purpose: Overcome skepticism using reviews and author background.*

### Row 4: 2-Column Split (60% / 40%)
*   **Column 1 (Left): Praise & Testimonials**
    *   *Element:* Custom Heading (H2). Text: "What Readers Are Saying"
    *   *Element:* Message Box / Testimonial element. Text: '"This book completely changed how I approach my daily workflow. A must-read!"' — *Reviewer Name*
    *   *Element:* Message Box / Testimonial element. Text: '"Objective, crisp, and directly to the point. No fluff."' — *Reviewer Name*
*   **Column 2 (Right): Meet the Author**
    *   *Element:* Custom Heading (H2). Text: "About the Author"
    *   *Element:* Single Image. Small, circular profile photo of yourself.
    *   *Element:* Text Block. A brief 3 to 4 sentence biography establishing your credibility and why you wrote this book.

---

## SECTION 4: Final Call to Action (The Closer)
*Purpose: One final, clean push to eliminate distractions and capture the sale.*

### Row 5: 1-Column Centered Block
*   **Column 1:**
    *   *Element:* Custom Heading (H2). Text: "Ready to Start Reading?"
    *   *Element:* Text Block. Text: "Available now in Digital, Paperback, and Audiobook formats."
    *   *Element:* Call to Action (CTA) Button. Text: "Buy on Amazon / Retailer".
    *   *Settings:* Extra Large size, vibrant color, centered alignment.
