What “impact” means here

Citation indicates a documented scholarly reference; methodological use requires evidence that an approach was used or extended; policy relevance denotes appearance in policy-oriented analysis, not proof of policy adoption; and independent validation is claimed only when specifically demonstrated.

Cases are identified by their documented relationship to the work. Inclusion does not imply institutional endorsement, partnership, or direct implementation.
